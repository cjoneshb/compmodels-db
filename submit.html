Submit a Study

Use this form to propose a new entry for the database. Mandatory fields are marked with *. Submissions are held for review before appearing in the verified database — you will receive a confirmation once your entry is processed.

Data quality note: For the Mechanism-derived? field, please code conservatively. “Yes” means the authors explicitly argued that the architecture formalizes the dominant mechanism as specified by the theory. “Partial” means some justification was given but mechanism dominance was not formally derived. “No” means the architecture was chosen by convention, software, or without theoretical rationale.
